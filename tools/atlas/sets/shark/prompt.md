A flat 2D hand-painted texture atlas for a very low-poly PS1-era GREAT WHITE SHARK,
1254x1254, painted directly over the supplied UV layout. This is a UV sheet,
NOT a 3D render, NOT an illustration. No perspective, no drop shadow, no
background scene, no border or frame.

**EVERY BLOCK IS LABELLED WITH ITS NAME. PAINT WHAT THE LABEL SAYS.** Small
unlabelled blocks share the colour of a labelled one: they are more of that
same part, paint them the same.

THE REFERENCE COLOURS ARE LABELS, NOT PAINT. Decide the palette from the
SUBJECT block alone.

**ALL BLOCKS MUST BE PAINTED**, edge to edge, the small ones too.

**THE EYE IS MARKED ON THE REFERENCE.** The black dot ringed in white inside BODY (near its right end) is the EYE, placed where the real animal's eye is. Paint the eye exactly there and exactly that big — the mark's centre is the pupil's centre — and paint over the mark itself (no white ring may survive). It must read clearly at game distance: a DARK PUPIL, the SUBJECT's eye colour around it, and a LIGHT rim (a pale lid or ring) round the outside, sharp-edged, never a smudge or a dot lost in the fur. Painted once, it is worn on both sides. No other eye anywhere on the sheet.

**THE MOUTH IS MARKED ON THE REFERENCE.** The black bar ringed in white inside BODY (near its right end) is the MOUTH LINE: the lower edge of the UPPER jaw, running from the corner of the mouth (left end) to the snout (right end). Paint a row of jagged pale cream triangular teeth along that whole line, pointing DOWN from it, with a thin dark-red gum line just above their roots. Below the line, the notch cut into the bottom-right of BODY is the gap of the open mouth; the strip under it is the LOWER jaw: pale belly colour, with a few teeth pointing UP along its top edge. NO teeth anywhere else on BODY — not on the snout tip, not on the top of the head, not on the cheek. Paint over the mark itself.

**THE BACKGROUND MUST BE PURE WHITE #FFFFFF** — not black, not transparent.
**Nothing painted may be white** — the palest tones are a dirty cream around
#E8DCC0 or a light grey.

**Fill each block edge to edge and a little past it.** Nothing may hang OUT of
a block. White holes inside a block are gaps in the model: paint around them.

Every block is HALF of the animal (painted once, worn on both sides) unless it
is a single centre piece. Seen from the side, the FRONT is at the RIGHT.

- FIN — a SHARK fin (the dorsal fin and the pectoral fins): smooth leathery shark SKIN, the same slate grey as the back, a darker trailing edge (the LEFT edge). NOT a fish fin: NO rays, NO ribs, NO stripes, NO veins, NO fan pattern.
- BODY — the WHOLE shark from the side in ONE continuous piece: the pointed snout and jaws at the RIGHT end (the EYE and MOUTH at their marks, the gill slits behind them), the tail stock at the LEFT, the back along the TOP edge, the belly along the BOTTOM edge. One unbroken skin from snout to tail: no seam or colour change where the head meets the body.
- SNOUT FRONT — the very tip of the snout seen HEAD-ON (its centre line is the LEFT edge, half of it): grey on top, the pale belly colour below. The black bar ringed in white across it is the MOUTH LINE seen from the front, at exactly the same height as the mouth on BODY: the dark red gum line with a few jagged pale teeth hanging DOWN from it, exactly along the mark, and pale lower jaw below. It must meet the BODY mouth line at the corner. No eye here.
- TAIL-FIN — the SHARK tail: two crescent lobes of smooth leathery shark SKIN, the same slate grey as the back, a darker rear edge. NOT a fish tail: NO rays, NO ribs, NO stripes, NO fan pattern.
- UPPER GUMS — the ROOF of the open mouth seen from below, half of it: dark blood-red gums and mouth interior, a row of pale cream triangular teeth along its BOTTOM edge pointing down. Its top edge is the centre line. Nothing else.
- LOWER GUMS — the FLOOR of the open mouth seen from above, half of it: dark blood-red gums, a row of pale cream teeth along its TOP edge pointing up. Nothing else.
- BODY: grey back above, pale belly below, the line between them crisp and continuous from the snout to the tail; the belly colour is also the lower jaw and the throat.

The UPPER GUMS / LOWER GUMS are the INSIDE of the open mouth: dark blood-red, with the teeth along their edge drawn as a few BIG clear triangles (not a fine checker of tiny teeth).

**ONE VALUE RANGE.** The body's blocks are one hide: no block lighter or
darker than the ones it meets.

SHADING, because this is a PS1-era game texture: broad and flat, light from
