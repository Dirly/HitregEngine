Generated by MMO/3d/Mobs/Dragon/work/make-prompts.mjs from the set's recipe.json
(Poly by Google source, CC-BY), then hand-edited (agent C, 2026-10-05): the
belly / tail underside / chest / back top / tail top got their own blocks so the

ABSOLUTE RULE, CHECK IT LAST: THE FINISHED SHEET CONTAINS NO TEXT. The
reference has words lettered inside the blocks (BODY, HEAD, TAIL, BELLY PALE, BACK TOP, CHEST, LEG, TAIL TOP, TEETH, CLAW, TAIL UNDER PALE, ARM). They are
instructions for YOU, not artwork: paint straight over them so that not one
letter survives.

A flat 2D hand-painted texture atlas for a very low-poly PS1-era TYRANNOSAUR,
1254x1254, painted directly over the supplied UV layout. This is a UV sheet,
NOT a 3D render, NOT an illustration. No perspective, no drop shadow, no
background scene, no border or frame.

**EVERY BLOCK IS LABELLED WITH ITS NAME. PAINT WHAT THE LABEL SAYS.** Small
unlabelled blocks share the colour of a labelled one: they are more of that
same part, paint them the same.

THE REFERENCE COLOURS ARE LABELS, NOT PAINT. Decide the palette from the
SUBJECT block alone.

**ALL BLOCKS MUST BE PAINTED**, edge to edge, the small ones too.

**THE EYE IS MARKED ON THE REFERENCE.** The black dot ringed in white high up
inside HEAD is the EYE. Paint the eye exactly there and at that size, NOT
smaller: a clear round eye with a burning amber iris, a dark slit pupil and a
thin light rim round it, so it reads from across a room. Paint over the mark
itself (no pure white ring may survive). Painted once, it is worn on both
sides. No other eye anywhere on the sheet.

**THE BACKGROUND MUST BE PURE WHITE #FFFFFF** — not black, not transparent.
**Nothing painted may be white** — the palest tones are a dirty cream around
#E8DCC0 or a light grey.

**Fill each block edge to edge and a little past it.** Nothing may hang OUT of
a block. White holes inside a block are gaps in the model: paint around them.

Every block is HALF of the animal (painted once, worn on both sides) unless it
is a single centre piece. Seen from the side, the FRONT is at the RIGHT.

- BODY — the trunk and thigh from the side, front at the RIGHT, the back along the TOP edge, the belly along the BOTTOM edge.
- CHEST — the front of the chest and neck base seen from the FRONT: neck at the TOP; the pale throat colour along the LEFT edge (the centre line), hide towards the right.
- BELLY PALE — the underside of the trunk seen from BELOW, entirely the pale belly colour; the centre line along the TOP edge, it shades only slightly darker towards the BOTTOM edge where it meets the flank.
- BACK TOP — the back seen from ABOVE: the dark dorsal ridge along the BOTTOM edge (the spine), the flank along the top edge.
- TAIL — the tail from the side: its thick base at the RIGHT where it joins the body, its tip at the LEFT; the back along the TOP edge, the pale underside along the BOTTOM edge.
- TAIL TOP — the top of the tail seen from ABOVE: the dark ridge along the BOTTOM edge, thick end at the RIGHT.
- TAIL UNDER PALE — the underside of the tail seen from BELOW, entirely the pale belly colour, thick end at the RIGHT.
- HEAD — the head from the side: the jaws at the RIGHT, the back of the skull at the LEFT, the EYE at its mark; the pale throat along the BOTTOM edge.
- LEG — the lower leg and foot laid flat: knee at the TOP, toes at the BOTTOM.
- ARM — a tiny arm, two claws at its tip; the hide colour above, the pale colour along its bottom edge.
- CLAW — the big toe claws: dark horn.
- TEETH — the teeth: jagged, yellowed (never white).

**ONE PALE UNDERSIDE.** The pale belly is ONE colour at ONE value everywhere
it appears: BELLY PALE, TAIL UNDER PALE, the bottom strip of BODY, the bottom
strip of TAIL, the bottom strip of HEAD (throat), the left edge of CHEST, the
inner/back edge of LEG where the thigh meets the belly, and the underside of
ARM. Mix it once and use that same paint on every one of those blocks: where
one block's pale edge meets another's on the model there must be no step in
colour or brightness. No block may have a pinker, greyer, lighter or darker
belly than another.

**ONE VALUE RANGE.** The body's blocks are one hide: no block lighter or
darker than the ones it meets. The hide on LEG, ARM and TAIL is the same hide
as BODY.

SHADING, because this is a PS1-era game texture: broad and flat, light from
ABOVE painted in, no photographic detail, no smooth gradients. At a quarter
size every block must still read as what it is.
