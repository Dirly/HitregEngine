ABSOLUTE RULE, CHECK IT LAST: THE FINISHED SHEET CONTAINS NO TEXT. The
reference has words lettered inside the blocks (TAIL, BODY, CHEST, RUMP, BACK TOP, HEAD, HEAD TOP, SNOUT FRONT, LEG-BACK, LEG-FRONT, EAR). They are
instructions for YOU, not artwork: paint straight over them so that not one
letter survives.

A flat 2D hand-painted texture atlas for a very low-poly PS1-era TIGER,
1254x1254, painted directly over the supplied UV layout. This is a UV sheet,
NOT a 3D render, NOT an illustration. No perspective, no drop shadow, no
background scene, no border or frame.

**EVERY BLOCK IS LABELLED WITH ITS NAME. PAINT WHAT THE LABEL SAYS.** Small
unlabelled blocks share the colour of a labelled one: they are more of that
same part, paint them the same.

THE REFERENCE COLOURS ARE LABELS, NOT PAINT. Decide the palette from the
SUBJECT block alone.

**ALL BLOCKS MUST BE PAINTED**, edge to edge, the small ones too.

**THE EYE IS MARKED ON THE REFERENCE.** The black dot ringed in white near the FRONT of HEAD, just behind where it meets the muzzle is the EYE. Paint the eye exactly there and at that size, NOT smaller: a clear round eye with a gold iris, a dark round pupil and a thin dark-ringed light rim, so it reads from across a room. Paint over the mark itself (no pure white ring may survive). Painted once, it is worn on both sides. No other eye anywhere on the sheet.

**THE BACKGROUND MUST BE PURE WHITE #FFFFFF** — not black, not transparent.
**Nothing painted may be white** — the palest tones are a dirty cream around
#E8DCC0 or a light grey.

**Fill each block edge to edge and a little past it.** Nothing may hang OUT of
a block. White holes inside a block are gaps in the model: paint around them.

Every block is HALF of the animal (painted once, worn on both sides) unless it
is a single centre piece. Seen from the side, the FRONT is at the RIGHT.

- TAIL — the tail laid flat, its base where it joins the body, its tip at the far end.
- BODY — the trunk from the side, front at the RIGHT, the back along the TOP edge, the belly along the BOTTOM edge.
- CHEST — the chest seen from the FRONT: neck at the TOP, between the forelegs at the BOTTOM; the throat and belly colour.
- RUMP — the back end seen from BEHIND: the tail root at the TOP centre, the haunches either side.
- BACK TOP — the back seen from ABOVE: the spine along one long edge, the flank along the other; half of the back.
- HEAD — the head from the side: the front (muzzle, beak or jaws) at the RIGHT, the back of the skull at the LEFT, the EYE at its mark.
- HEAD TOP — the top of the head seen from ABOVE, the front pointing RIGHT; half of it. No eye, no face.
- SNOUT FRONT — the very front of the muzzle seen head-on: the nose and the top of the lips; half of it.
- LEG-BACK — a hind leg from outside: hip at the TOP, paw or hoof at the BOTTOM.
- LEG-FRONT — a fore leg from outside: shoulder at the TOP, paw or hoof at the BOTTOM.
- EAR — an ear, the inside darker.

**ONE VALUE RANGE.** The body's blocks are one hide: no block lighter or
darker than the ones it meets.

SHADING, because this is a PS1-era game texture: broad and flat, light from
ABOVE painted in, no photographic detail, no smooth gradients. At a quarter
size every block must still read as what it is.

SUBJECT:
A huge MENACING tiger: burning orange fur (#C8641C) with bold black stripes, cream belly, chin and inner legs (#E0D4B8, never white), black-and-cream ears, a ringed tail; a gold EYE.

FINAL CHECK: no letters anywhere; every block painted; the background pure white; nothing painted white; the eye is clear and at its mark.
